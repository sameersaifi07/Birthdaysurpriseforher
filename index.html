Questions

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. It's a website, so it opens in any phone's browser: no app to install.

Can I use my own song?

Yes. Add any song you have the right to use; it starts when they tap the first heart.

Who can see my photos?

You, your partner (with the link and the PIN), and our support team, who can open your website to help you or fix a problem. The published website is encrypted, so the link alone shows nothing.

Does my partner need to log in?

No. Only you log in to build it. Your partner just opens the link and types the PIN.

Can it unlock exactly at midnight?

Yes. You choose the date and time; until then they see a countdown, then the PIN screen appears by itself.

How do I put it online?

You don't need to. Log in, build it in your studio and tap Publish: you get a private link to send. With “We make it for you”, we build it and send you the link.

Can I change things after it's made?

Yes. Edit in your studio and tap Publish again: the same link updates. With our done-for-you plans, changes are included as described.
